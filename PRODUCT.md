# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Gamer, developers, podcasters, and workstation users on Arch/CachyOS who need a high-fidelity, low-latency microphone connection from their Android phone to their Linux machine.

## Product Purpose
To provide a native, open-source, lossless audio link streaming raw 48kHz PCM over TCP directly into the PipeWire virtual audio graph, offering a reliable and zero-overhead replacement for proprietary closed-source alternatives.

## Brand Personality
* **Tactical Gear**: Feels like a dedicated hardware audio interface or cyberpunk deck.
* **Futuristic/Electric**: Utilizes dark backdrops with hot neon status accents.
* **Slick & Precise**: Fluid UI transitions, micro-animations, and high-fidelity indicators.

## Anti-references
* **Material Design Defaults**: Avoid generic light blue grids and standard system borders that feel like a generic spreadsheet app.
* **SaaS Bloat**: Avoid massive headers, marketing cards, or complex account/login flows. This is a utilities-first application.
* **Flat Monotony**: Avoid flat, structureless grey sheets. Depth must be conveyed with borders and neon glow states.

## Design Principles
* **Raw Visual Feedback**: Keep audio meters highly responsive, utilizing multi-color gradients (green to hot pink) to show input gains.
* **Visual Rarity**: Use neon accents (cyan and pink) sparingly (≤15% of the screen) to signify active states and errors.
* **Tactile Interactions**: Buttons and tabs must respond to touch/hover with subtle lighting changes or scale transitions, mimicking physical buttons.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
* **Contrast**: High-contrast text on dark backgrounds to ensure legibility in low-light environments (common for gamers/night workers).
* **Reduced Motion**: Respect system preferences for reduced animations, disabling pulsing ring visualizers if requested.
